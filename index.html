PROBLEM STATEMENT

[01]

Niche & Local Isolation

People have unique hobbies (photography, sketching, board games) but struggle to locate compatible individuals within their local geographical radius.

[02]

Constraint Friction

Matching manually across 4 strict dimensions — Shared Interest, Overlapping Free Time, Proximity Radius, and Budget Expectations — is nearly impossible.

[03]

The Invisible Latent Void

Significant local demand exists in a neighborhood, but it remains 100% invisible because no single user takes the friction-heavy leap to organize a new group.

THE FUNDAMENTAL MISSING QUESTION IN EXISTING APPS

“Existing platforms ask: 'Where can I find a group?'
Our platform asks: 'Why doesn't a group exist for something that 20 people nearby want to do right now?'”

THE CORE INNOVATION

TRADITIONAL MODEL (DISCOVERY-DRIVEN)

  • 1. Existing Group Created by Organizer
  • 2. User Searches & Browses Directory
  • 3. User Joins Pre-Existing Community
  • ❌ FAILS when no group exists for niche interests!
  • ❌ Requires manual organizer initiative upfront.

OUR MODEL (DEMAND-DRIVEN — USP)

  • 1. User Inputs Interests & Availability Constraints
  • 2. System Detects Latent Local Demand Clusters
  • 3. System Identifies 'Community Gap' (No Group Exists)
  • 4. Auto-Generates Group & Matches Compatible Users
  • 5. Auto-Recommends Venue, Optimal Time & Activity Plan
  • ✅ Works even with zero prior organizers!
  • ✅ Turns passive interest into real-world meetups.

TECHNICAL APPROACH & ALGORITHM

CONCEPTUAL MATCHING & COMPATIBILITY SCORE FORMULA
Score = w₁·S(Interest) + w₂·S(Proximity) + w₃·S(TimeOverlap) + w₄·S(Budget) + w₅·S(Activity)
Calculated across candidate clusters to trigger Community Gap Detection when score threshold & group size constraints are met.

1. Spatial & Temporal

• Density-Based Spatial Clustering (DBSCAN / H3 Index) for location bounding.
• Overlapping Interval Trees for discovering common availability windows.

2. Multi-Constraint

• Filters budget mismatches (e.g. ₹200 vs ₹2000 expectations).
• Calculates max participant attendance time slots within travel radius.

3. Targeted AI

• LLM prompt-synthesis for natural-language activity suggestions & venue descriptions.
• Dynamic rationale generation: 'Why this group was formed'.
